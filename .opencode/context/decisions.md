# Decisions

- [binding] Reseller data models must not leak into core domain; translate at boundary. — See `docs/00-method/02-ddd.md` (verified 2026-11-06)
- [binding] Coins are the internal unit; storefront/checkout pricing derives coin cost from USD price via the coin index (`coin:index_micro`) with persisted cap/run state. — See `src/treasury/domain/coin-index.ts`, `src/treasury/application/coin-index-settings.ts`, `src/api/pricing.ts`, `src/api/checkout.ts`, `docs/01-domain/04-pricing-markup.md` §Coin index (verified 2026-11-07)
- [binding] Treasury tracks WAC, funding lots carrying total EGP paid at record time, and consumption; coin value derives from the coin index — there is no separate coin regime. — See `src/treasury/domain/wac.ts`, `src/treasury/domain/coin-index.ts`, `src/treasury/application/service.ts`, `src/treasury/domain/funding.ts` (verified 2026-11-07)
- [binding] P2P rate integration (Binance P2P) provides market rate for treasury/dashboard with manual override support; per-method medians (bank transfer, Vodafone Cash) drive headline as max with median of top 10 cheapest ads. Ads beyond the configured outlier sigma (default 2, persisted `p2p:outlier_sigma`, canonical bounds in `src/shared/p2p-settings.ts`; population stddev) from the mean are excluded before both medians and the combined range, which carries min/average/max + ad count (average HALF-UP) plus population stddev and outlier count over all bank-or-Vodafone ads without double-counting dual-method ads; the range travels in `AdminDashboardDto.p2p` (aggregated over the configured observation window; the live quote's combined range is carried into history points). Base eligibility/parsing (non-promoted, ≥200 USDT tradable, integer-minor price) is canonical in `eligibility.ts`, re-exported by `quote.ts`;
  `currentEgpPerUsdtMinor` is the cheapest base-eligible Vodafone offer with no sigma filter, and `allEligibleStats` (`P2pFetchStats`, sigma-filtered) spans every base-eligible ad by any method. Both exist only on `P2pQuote`/`P2pMethodQuote`; `P2pRate` and history carry headline/per-method/range only. — See `src/integration/binance-p2p/eligibility.ts`, `src/integration/binance-p2p/quote.ts`, `src/treasury/ports/p2p-quote.ts`, `src/treasury/application/p2p-rate.ts`, `src/api/contracts/admin.ts`, `src/api/admin.ts` (verified 2026-11-08)
- [binding] VIP markup: per-game `LevelSet`/curve (basis points, non-increasing) with per-client override taking precedence. Resolution uses qualifying volume over a rolling 30×24h window: coin deposits plus delivered orders not paid with coins; orders snapshot `markupBasisPoints` and `coinsConsumed`. Earned level badges are persistent: the first earn for (clientId, levelKey) wins, badges survive demotion, and `recordLevelAchievements` evaluation is lazy and idempotent. The allowed level icons/colors are canonical in `src/shared/level-badge.ts`, shared by the ordering domain and the management VIP editor; add new icons/colors there, not in a subsystem copy. — See `src/ordering/domain/level.ts`, `src/ordering/domain/vip-markup.ts`, `src/ordering/application/vip-activity.ts`, `src/ordering/ports/activity-repository.ts`, `src/infrastructure/prisma/activity-repository.ts`, `src/ordering/application/levels.ts`, `src/ordering/ports/level-achievement-repository.ts`, `src/infrastructure/prisma/level-achievement-repository.ts`, `src/shared/level-badge.ts`, `src/app/management/(shell)/vip-curve-editor.tsx` (verified 2026-11-07)
- [binding] Order snapshots financial state at placement (cost, discount, promo, WAC/COGS, trading margin, coins consumed/charged, markup basis points) for reporting and refund integrity. — See `src/ordering/domain/order.ts`, `src/ordering/application/place-order.ts`, `src/api/checkout.ts` (verified 2026-11-06)
- [binding] There is no pending-funding state: every funding lot is appended with a positive total EGP paid (including fees) and USDT received, and the snapshot is funding totals minus consumption totals, so a lot enters WAC as soon as it is recorded. — See `src/treasury/domain/funding.ts`, `src/treasury/application/service.ts`, `src/treasury/ports/funding-repository.ts`, `src/infrastructure/prisma/treasury-repository.ts`, `prisma/schema.prisma` (verified 2026-11-07)
- [binding] Coin deposits accept only EGP or USDT (stored as `USD`) and are manual entries on `/management/coins`; Instapay and Crypto gateways are not live, and any other currency is rejected at the route, server action, and form. — See `docs/01-domain/06-credits.md` §Deposits and rates, `docs/03-specs/06-back-office.md` §Customer coin deposits (`/management/coins`), `src/app/api/admin/deposit/route.ts`, `src/app/management/(shell)/coins/actions.ts`, `src/app/management/(shell)/coins/purchase-modal.tsx` (verified 2026-11-07)
- [binding] Coin value is the coin index in USD minor (1.0 = 1 cent per coin); deposit rates derive from it — USDT deposits at the coin value, EGP at the coin value × `max(WAC, market)` with a basis-point fee applied half-up and invalid stored values falling back to defaults. — See `docs/01-domain/04-pricing-markup.md` §Coin index, `docs/01-domain/06-credits.md` §Deposits and rates, `src/treasury/application/deposit-settings.ts`, `src/treasury/domain/coin-index.ts` (verified 2026-11-07)
